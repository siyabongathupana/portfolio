Portfolio User Guide

Updated for Cloudflare OTP authentication and private GitHub storage. Find the features you used before—without shared GitHub tokens or public user data.

Account access

Get an invitation

Ask the portfolio owner to invite your email. They must also authorise that exact email in Cloudflare Access.

Sign in with OTP

Open the protected Timesheet link. Enter the invited email address and the one-time PIN sent to that inbox. Never share the PIN.

Activate a new account

If you received an invitation to create an account, open Account Activation while signed in with your own email. Existing users generally do not need activation.

No GitHub tokens required

Employees do not need a GitHub account, Personal Access Token or shared portfolio password.

Timesheets and analytics

Timesheet dashboard

Use Secure Timesheets to add, edit, search or export entries, subject to your permissions.

My Analytics

Open My Analytics for your own totals, charts and reports.

Offline JSON viewer

Use Offline Timesheet Viewer to inspect a JSON export locally. It does not save to your account or contact the private API.

Studies and reports

Study Manager

Open Study Manager if you have permission. Your records remain private.

Study Reports

Open Study Reports for charts, progress and reporting.

Shared studies

If the owner has invited you to a read-only academic view, use Shared Studies or Shared Study Reports. Shared access may expire or be revoked.

Projects and certificates

Public galleries

Anyone can browse approved Projects and Certificates without accessing private records.

Manage private projects

Authorised users can open Project Management and generate their internal PDF reports.

Manage certificates

Authorised owners can open Certificate Management. Public certificates and thumbnail images are separately approved.

Messaging and administration

My messages

Use My Messages to read private portfolio messages and respond.

Administration

Only authorised owners can access Admin, User Management, Admin Messaging and Admin Analytics.

Permissions

An OTP verifies email ownership; it does not grant every feature. Permissions are checked by the backend for each protected operation.

Privacy and troubleshooting

Private data

Timesheets, user records, studies and management data are stored in a private repository, accessed through an authenticated Cloudflare Worker.

Missing OTP

Check Spam and confirm the exact email is included in the application Cloudflare Access Allow policy.

Access denied

Ask the owner to confirm your invitation, permission and account status. Do not share your OTP or any access credentials.

Backups

The administrator manages backups of private data. Public GitHub Pages does not hold the private user records.
