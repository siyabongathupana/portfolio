Questions worth asking

What people usually wonder

Do I need an account to explore YourPortfolio?

Public projects, public certificates and this guide are available to visitors. To save personal records or work privately, request an account and wait for approval.

Does having a portfolio account automatically unlock Siya?

No. Siya AI access is a separate permission. You can request it once your portfolio account is set up.

Will Siya tell me information about other users?

Not unless your account is legitimately permitted to access that particular information. Permissions are enforced by the secure service, not just by the robot's personality.

Does Siya grow just because I open a page?

No. Growth is intentionally slow and tied to meaningful authorized use over time. The exact progression is kept as a surprise. You cannot buy or force a stage through the guide.

Can Siya replace an engineer, tutor or supervisor?

Siya can help you reason, learn and organize work, but it can make mistakes. Important engineering, safety, financial and academic decisions need independent checks.

What if something isn't working?

Describe the issue to Siya if you have access. If you don't, you can still read this guide and use the account/request-access link. If your secure session has expired, you may need to reconnect deliberately.

Are weekly email reminders required?

No. This version focuses on in-app workflows. Automated weekly Timesheet reminder emails are not enabled.
